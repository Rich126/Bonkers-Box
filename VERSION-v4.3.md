# Spencer Games v4.3 — Speedway Final Race Replay

## Speedway Slide
- Records each rider’s real position, heading, speed and progress during their solo three-lap run.
- After the final rider, **Watch everyone race!** launches a combined replay with all riders on track together.
- Replay timing and finishing order come from the actual recorded solo runs.
- Small visual lane offsets keep bikes readable when riders take similar lines.
- Live replay leader and running order are shown during the race.
- Final leaderboard includes **Watch the race again** for instant replay.
- Existing LEFT / RIGHT captured touch controls remain unchanged.
