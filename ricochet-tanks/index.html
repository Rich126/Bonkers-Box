FIELD MANUAL

Desktop

WASD or arrow keys to move. Mouse to aim, hold click or Space to fire. E drops a mine. P or Esc pauses.

Phone / tablet

Drag the left stick to move. Drag and hold the right stick to aim and fire. Tap MINE, then move away. Landscape gives you more room.

Three hull points. Three mines per mission. At most three of your shells in flight. Stone stops tanks; crates absorb one shell. Mines arm after one second and explode near any tank or after seven seconds. Explosions respect walls. Friendly fire is on.
