# Spencer Games v4.0 — Draw Dash Gold Edition

## Draw Dash overhaul
- Replaces the five near-identical circuit silhouettes with five deliberately different Grand Prix layouts.
- Adds venue-specific scenery for British, Italian, Ardennes, Harbour Street and Desert Night events.
- Adds proper kerbs, start complex, pit branding and richer circuit rendering.
- Smooths raw finger traces for race playback while preserving the player's recorded throttle and validated lap crossings.
- Adds look-ahead steering and faster pose interpolation for less clunky car movement.
- Adds a subtle dynamic race camera focused on the leading pack.
- Adds redesigned vector race cars, speed trails, boost flames, drafting particles and contact effects.
- Adds five-light race start, GO overlay, live race HUD, overtake callouts, chequered-flag sequence, confetti and podium-style results.
- Improves live position cards and photo-finish presentation.
