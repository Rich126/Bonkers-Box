GAME GUIDE

  
Choose a game that suits the group, then follow the instructions shown on screen.

  

   
Games included

   

    
🏁 Draw Dash GoldDraw the entire selected race in one continuous stroke. Finger speed becomes throttle, then watch your route race through one of 5 distinct cinematic Grand Prix circuits.

    
🏍️ Speedway SlideEach rider takes one turn at a three-lap shale time trial. The bike accelerates automatically; hold LEFT to turn in and RIGHT to catch the slide. Grass and the safety fence scrub speed. After the last run, every recorded ride is replayed together as one final race before the results.

    
💡 Flash Frenzy!Each player gets the same sequence of lights. Tap green as quickly as possible. In Frenzy mode, ignore red decoys — false starts and red taps add time penalties. Lowest total reaction time wins.

    
🌊 Treasure DivePush your luck by diving deeper for treasure. Stop safely or risk meeting a monster and losing what you found.

    
🧠 Noodle NogginWatch an emoji sequence, remember it, then tap the same sequence back in the correct order.

    
⚡ Zappy TappyA reaction game. Tap the correct target before it disappears. Fastest accurate players score best.

    
🩳 Smarty PantsAn age-adjusted multiple-choice quiz. Questions are chosen to give children and adults a fair challenge.

    
🍿 Popcorn PuzzlerWork out the film represented by the emoji clue, then choose the correct answer.

    
🔍 Peek-a-Who?A Guess Who-style game. Ask yes-or-no questions to eliminate characters and identify the mystery person or animal.

    
🎨 Doodle DuelDraw the prompt, pass the device, then vote for the best or funniest picture.

    
🗡️ Traitor’s TableA social deduction game for 3–6 players. Read secret information privately, discuss the clues and vote for the suspected traitor.

    
🏔️ Avalanche!Roll to climb the mountain. Keep rolling for more progress or stop and secure a camp. Rolling a 1 can send you sliding back.

    
👦 Kids vs ParentsA team quiz. Children receive suitable questions, adults receive harder ones, and both teams compete for the highest score.

    
💀 Guess or DieA dramatic word-guessing game. Guess letters and solve the word before too many wrong guesses are made.

    
💣 Bomb PassComplete the spoken challenge, then pass the phone quickly. Do not be holding it when the hidden timer explodes.

    
👉 Who’s Most Likely?Read a statement, then everyone points or votes for the person who best matches it.

   

  

  
For younger players
An adult should read longer prompts aloud. There is no need to rush unless the game specifically shows a timer.
